    IN-GAME ACTIONS (ON-CHAIN)

    Same rules as Practice Mode, driven by the contract: 10-second spin, raises of +20 SQK with a 30-second decision window and a separate 60-second transaction window. The spin is automatic: with Auto-spin on, this page sends the spin by itself the moment the contract's 10 seconds are up — MetaMask just asks you to confirm each round. The Spin button stays locked with a countdown until the spin is actually ready, so an early press can never send a transaction that fails. Anyone can call Open Tx Window / Settle once the contract's timers allow it — and this page now calls them automatically: the moment the 30-second decision window ends (deadline passed, or every alive player has decided) it sends Open Tx Window by itself, and the moment the 60-second transaction window ends (deadline passed, or every accepted transaction is settled) it sends Settle Raise by itself, so a raise can no longer sit stuck waiting for a manual click. The manual buttons below stay as a fallback.

    Auto-spin ON — spins fire by themselves each round; you only confirm in MetaMask.
    Join a game and the spin status will show here.

    Confirm raise tx also approves 20 SQK first if your allowance is too low. Treasury draw works once 1,800 blocks have passed since the last draw and the treasury holds SQK.

    ⚡ RAISE PROPOSED

    The 30-second timer above is only for accepting or declining — no answer counts as a decline, and the timer ends the moment every alive player has decided. Accepting is not paying yet: if you accept, a separate 60-second transaction window opens below for submitting and confirming your +20 SQK transaction, and it too ends early once every accepted transaction is confirmed or failed. Raise limit depends on the lobby — 1 raise in a 4-player game, 2 in a 9-player game, 3 in a 16-player game — always +20 SQK, never more.

    ⛓ TRANSACTION WINDOW — UP TO 60 SECONDS

    Everyone who accepted now submits their +20 SQK raise transaction on Sepolia and waits for it to confirm. The window ends early once every accepted transaction is confirmed or failed. Anyone whose transaction is not confirmed when this timer ends is eliminated.

    In practice mode this simulates the Sepolia confirmation — on-chain, your wallet signs the transaction and the contract waits for it to land.

        HOW IT PLAYS

        1. Square lobbies only. A game starts with exactly 4 (2×2), 9 (3×3) or 16 (4×4) players — one hamster tile each. Any other count can never form the board.
        2. Entry is 20 SQK. Of every entry, 18 SQK goes into the final tile's prize and 2 SQK is the game fee, paid to the creator's address. Starting pots: 72 SQK (4 players), 162 SQK (9 players), 288 SQK (16 players).
        3. The wheel decides. The hamster runs, the wheel spins for 10 seconds, then stops and one address is picked at random from the players still alive and eliminated. Repeat until one tile remains.
        4. The final tile has 3 tiers, locked at game start by the block-seed formula above: FULL (50%) the survivor takes the whole pot · HALF (30%) half to the survivor, half to the treasury · EMPTY (20%) the whole pot goes to the treasury.
        5. Raises — decision first. After an elimination, any surviving player can propose a raise. Everyone still alive gets 30 seconds to accept or decline the same +20 SQK — that window is only for deciding, and it ends immediately once every alive player has accepted or declined. Declining (or not answering) eliminates you on that tile.
        6. Raises — then the transaction window. Everyone who accepted gets a separate 60 seconds to submit and confirm their +20 SQK transaction on Sepolia (18 to the pot, 2 fee). Each player's status is shown live — pending / confirmed / failed — the window ends immediately once every accepted transaction is confirmed or failed, and anyone not confirmed when it ends is eliminated. The raise limit depends on the lobby: 1 raise in a 4-player game, 2 in a 9-player game, 3 in a 16-player game — always the entry amount, so nobody can price others out with a bigger raise.
        7. Treasury airdrop. Every 1,800 blocks on Sepolia (about 12 seconds per block, so roughly every 6 hours) anyone can trigger the draw: everything the treasury has collected is raffled among all addresses that entered a game before that draw block, and airdropped to one winner.
        8. On-chain: connect a wallet, approve SQK, then send the 20 SQK entry — the join transaction is recorded by the contract on Sepolia. The contract at the bottom of this page is the real thing this demo mirrors.

        THE CONTRACT — SqueekTiles.sol (Sepolia · SQK)

        The on-chain version of exactly the game above: wallet connect → approve SQK → joinLobby() records the 20 SQK entry transaction. The tier formula, raise rules (lobby raise limit of 1 / 2 / 3 × entry amount, a 30-second decision window that ends early once everyone has accepted or declined, plus a separate 60-second transaction window that ends early once every accepted transaction is confirmed or failed), creator fee and the treasury draw every 1,800 blocks (about 6 hours) all live in the contract, not on a server.
